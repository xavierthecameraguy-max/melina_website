# Google Sites build kit

Milena edits her website herself in Google Sites (click and type, no code, no AI, $0), so the Sunny Passport
design is rebuilt there by hand, in her own Google account, on onyxworldtravel.com. This folder is everything
needed for that. Start with `1-build-guide.md`.

- `1-build-guide.md`: Xavier's step-by-step build, from Milena's Google account to connecting the domain at GoDaddy.
- `2-text-to-paste.md`: every word on every page, section by section. `[TO FILL: ...]` marks what Milena still has to supply.
- `3-forms-and-buttons.md`: the three Google Forms (each one emails Milena), every button and link and where it goes,
  and the "no fake buttons" test to run before launch.
- `4-milena-guide.md`: Milena's own guide to editing the site. Give her the PDF at hand-over, after checking the
  button names listed at its end.
- `images/`: every picture, renamed for where it goes, with `images/INDEX.md` giving its section and alt text.
  Metadata is stripped. The same pictures are zipped as `onyx-images.zip` for uploading.
- `pdf/`: the four guides and the picture list as printable PDFs.

Anything marked "(check in the editor)" or UNVERIFIED comes from research that couldn't open a signed-in editor;
confirm it in the live editor (the full list is in the build guide's Appendix A).
