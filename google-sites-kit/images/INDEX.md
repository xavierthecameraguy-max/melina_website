# Onyx World Travel: picture list for Google Sites

This folder has every picture for the Onyx World Travel website, ready to upload to Google Sites. The list below says which page and which section each picture goes on, and what to type as its **alt text**. The section names match `2-text-to-paste.md`. For example, "Home 1. Hero" is the first section of the Home page.

**What is alt text?** A short description of the picture. People who can't see the screen hear it read out loud, and Google reads it too.

## How to use these pictures in Google Sites

- **Add a picture:** in the editor, Insert (right panel) > Images > Upload, then choose the file from this folder. Drag it into place and drag its corner dots to resize it.
- **Add alt text:** click the picture > More (three dots) > Add alt text, then paste the alt text from the list below.
- **Logo and tab icon:** these don't go on a page. They go in Settings (gear, top right) > Brand images > Logo, and Favicon.
- **The drawings (.png)** have a see-through background, so they sit nicely on the light sections: Cream and Sunshine. Don't put them on a Dark section, where the black outlines would disappear. The postcard goes on **Cream only**, because its pale sun fades into Sunshine. Whether Google Sites keeps the see-through background is UNVERIFIED, so check it once in Preview.
- **The number and icon pictures** are 240 x 240 pixels. Show them small, about 80 to 100 pixels wide, like a large coin.
- **Photos (.jpg)** are at most 1600 pixels on the long side, which is about the most Google Sites keeps (it shrinks bigger pictures; LIKELY). The hidden camera and location details have been removed.
- **Tall photos** (about-1-milena.jpg, contact-1-milena.jpg): after placing them, check in Preview on a computer and on a phone that her face is fully in view.
- Anything in **[TO FILL: ...]** is still waiting on Milena. Fill it in before publishing.
- Keep a copy of this folder somewhere safe, outside Google. It's the spare copy of all the website's pictures.
