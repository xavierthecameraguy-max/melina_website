# GoDaddy build kit

Milena will edit her site herself in GoDaddy's website builder (click and type, no code), so the
Sunny Passport design is rebuilt there by hand. This folder is everything needed for that:

- `02-build-guide.md`: Xavier's step-by-step build in GoDaddy's Websites + Marketing builder (start with Step 0).
- `01-text-to-paste.md`: every word on every page, section by section. `[TO FILL: ...]` marks what Milena still has to supply.
- `03-forms-and-buttons.md`: each form's fields (all send to milena@onyxworldtravel.com), every button and where it goes,
  and the "no fake buttons" test to run before launch.
- `04-milena-guide.md`: Milena's own guide to editing the site (give her the PDF after confirming the button names
  listed at its end).
- `images-INDEX.md` and `graphics/`: which picture goes where, with alt text. The postcard, icons, step badges and logo
  are here; the photos are in `src/assets/photos/` (renamed copies are in the kit's zip).

Anything marked "(check in the editor)" comes from research that couldn't open godaddy.com; confirm it in the live editor.
