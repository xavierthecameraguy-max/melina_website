# Your website: how to make changes

**Milena's guide to editing onyxworldtravel.com**

Hi Milena!

This guide is yours to keep. It shows you, step by step, how to change your website yourself: the words, the photos, your trips and your reviews. You do it all in GoDaddy by clicking and typing, a bit like filling in a form. You don't need any special skills.

Three things to know before you start:

- **Looking around can't break anything.** Your changes save by themselves as you go, but nobody else sees them until you click **Publish**.
- **There's a safety net.** GoDaddy keeps backup copies of your website, and you can go back to one (section 14).

Each section below is one job. Find the job you want and follow the steps in order.

## A few words you'll see

- **Editor:** the screen where you make changes to your website.
- **Panel:** the column on the right side of the editor. When you click something on your page, its options show up there.
- **Section:** one band of a page, running across the whole page. For example, "How it works" on your Home page is one section.
- **Card:** one box in a row of boxes, like one trip under "Where are we going next?".
- **Preview:** shows your changes the way visitors will see them. Nothing goes live.
- **Publish:** puts your changes on your real website, for everyone.
- **Hidden page:** a page that isn't in your menu. Your trip pages are hidden pages: people reach them from the trip cards.

## 1. Open your website editor

1. Go to godaddy.com and click **Sign In**. Type your username and password.
2. Go to **My Products**.
3. Scroll down to **Websites + Marketing**.
4. Next to your website, click **Manage**.
5. Click **Edit Website**. (On some screens it says **Edit Site**.)

You're in! Your website is in the middle of the screen, and the panel is on the right.

**To go to another page:** in the panel on the right, click **Site Navigation**, then click the page's name.

Good to know:

- Use a computer for the jobs in this guide. The GoDaddy app on your phone is fine for changing a word or a photo, but bigger jobs are much easier on a computer.
- You never need to click Save. Your changes save by themselves.
- If you get lost, you can simply close the window. Your work is saved, and nothing is live until you publish.
- Never click **Try a new look**. It changes the design of your whole website.


## 2. Change any words

1. Open your editor (section 1) and go to the page.
2. Click the words you want to change.
3. Type your new words.
   - To make words bold, highlight them and click **B** in the small bar of buttons that pops up.
   - For a list with dots, use the list button in that same bar.
4. **Words on a button:** click the button and change its words in the panel on the right. Leave where it goes alone, unless this guide tells you to change it.
5. If clicking some words doesn't let you type, click the section once and look for those words in the panel on the right.
6. Check your work (section 4), then **Publish** (section 5).

**Square brackets.** If you see something like "[TO FILL: how fast I reply]", that's a spot Xavier left for you, because only you know the answer. Click it, delete everything from the [ to the ], and type the real words. If you don't have the answer yet, leave it, but don't publish a page where visitors would see it.

Tip: typing words straight in is easiest. Words pasted from an email or a Word document can sometimes come in looking different from the rest of the page.

**Once a year, in January:** look at the very bottom of your website. If the year after the © is last year, click it, type the new year, and **Publish**.


## 3. Swap a photo

1. Open your editor and go to the page.
2. Click the photo.
3. In the panel on the right, click **Change Image**.
4. Click **Upload** and pick the photo on your computer.
5. If you see a box to describe the photo, type one short sentence about what's in it. For example: "Four friends smiling at the rim of a red-rock canyon". People who can't see the screen hear this read out loud, and Google reads it too.
6. Look in **Preview** to make sure nothing important is cut off, like someone's head.
7. **Publish** (section 5).

**The big photo at the top of a page** works a little differently: click the top of the page, then in the panel on the right click **Cover Media**, then **Change Image**.

Good to know:

- Photos straight from your phone are fine. You don't need to make them smaller first.
- JPG and PNG photos work. If a photo won't upload, it may be a kind GoDaddy doesn't take (some iPhone photos are). Try emailing it to yourself from your phone, then save it from the email and upload that copy. That often fixes it.
- A few pictures are special drawings Xavier made: the postcard at the top of your Home page, and the little yellow 1-2-3 numbers and icons. Leave those as they are. You can swap the postcard for an ordinary photo, but it would lose its stamp and its tilt.
- Only post photos of travelers who've said it's OK.


## 4. Check before you publish

A two-minute check saves headaches. Before every **Publish**:

1. Click **Preview** and look at the page the way a visitor would.
2. Switch to the phone view too. Many of your travelers will visit on their phones.
3. Read what you changed: names, dates, prices, spelling.
4. Look for any [square brackets] you meant to fill in.
5. Click every button and link you added or changed. Each one must do something real:
   - open the right page, or
   - start a call to your number, or
   - start an email to you.

   If one doesn't, fix it or delete it. A button that goes nowhere makes people think your website is broken.
6. Close **Preview** to go back to the editor.

If you changed a form or added a trip, also send yourself a test after you publish (section 13, part C).


## 5. Publish your changes

1. Click **Publish**. (On some screens it says **Publish Site**.)
2. Wait a moment until GoDaddy has finished.
3. Open onyxworldtravel.com to see your change.

Good to know:

- Every change needs a new **Publish**. Until then, only you can see it, in the editor.
- If you don't see your change yet, wait a few minutes and refresh the page, or look on your phone. You can also open a private window: in Chrome or Edge, hold down Ctrl and Shift and press N. On a Mac, hold down Command and Shift and press N. Then go to onyxworldtravel.com in that window.
- Each time you publish, GoDaddy keeps a backup copy of your website by itself (section 14).

## 6. Add a new trip

This is the biggest job in this guide, so pick a quiet time and go slowly. You'll do five things: copy your trip template, fill it in, add the trip's card on two pages, then publish and test.

**Before you start**

- Make a safety copy (section 14, part A). Name it something like "Before Italy 2027".
- Have the trip's details ready: the name, place and dates; how many days; the price, and the price for a room to yourself; the deposit and when the rest is due; group size and spots; activity level and walking; the book-by date; the day-by-day plan; what's included and what isn't; before-you-go notes; a couple of questions people ask; and a nice photo.

### Part A: Copy your trip template

1. Open your editor (section 1).
2. In the panel on the right, click **Site Navigation**.
3. Find the page called **Trip template**. Point at it with your mouse, without clicking. A small gear appears. Click the gear.
4. Click **Duplicate Page**. A copy appears in the list.
5. Click the gear next to the copy. Change its name to the trip's name, for example "Italy 2027". Keep it short, because the name becomes part of the trip's web address.
6. In the same place, make sure both menu switches (the top menu and the bottom menu) are **off**. Trip pages stay out of your menu. People find them from the trip cards.
7. Also in the same place, under **Get found on Google**, type a title like "Italy 2027 | Onyx World Travel" and one or two sentences about the trip. This is what people see when they find the trip on Google.

Never fill in or delete **Trip template** itself. It's your master copy for every future trip.

### Part B: Fill in the new trip page

1. In **Site Navigation**, click your new page (for example "Italy 2027") to open it. Make sure you're on the new page, not on Trip template.
2. Work down the page from top to bottom, and replace every [TO FILL: ...] with the real details:
   - **Top of the page:** the trip name and the one-line teaser. Delete the small "Sample trip" label. Change the big photo (section 3, "The big photo at the top of a page").
   - **Quick facts:** every line.
   - **Why I picked this trip.**
   - **Day by day:** one small heading and a short paragraph for each day. Add or delete days to match your trip.
   - **What's included / Not included:** always keep "Milena, along for the whole trip".
   - **Before you go.**
   - **Questions about this trip.**
3. The form at the bottom, **Save my spot**:
   1. Change the line "Trip: ..." to the trip's name and dates.
   2. Click the form. In the panel on the right, click **Edit Form Fields**.
   3. Click the field called **Trip**. Change its one choice to the trip's name and dates, for example: "Italy 2027, June 1 to 10, 2027". This tells you which trip each message is about.
   4. Check that **Send Message To** says milena@onyxworldtravel.com.
4. Click **Preview** and click both **Save my spot** buttons. Each one should take you down to the form on this same page. If one goes anywhere else, go back to the editor, click that button, and in the panel on the right set it to go to this page's "Save my spot" section.

### Part C: Add the trip's card on your Home page

1. Go to your Home page and find "Where are we going next?".
2. Click that section.
3. Make a new card:
   - If there's already a trip card there, copy it: in the panel on the right, find that card and click **Duplicate**.
   - If there's no trip card yet, click **Add Group** in the panel on the right to make a new card.
4. Use the arrows to move the new card so it comes before "More trips are on the way". That card always stays last.
5. Fill in the card the same way as the others: the trip's photo, the trip name, the small label "Spots open", the place and dates, the price per person, and the words "See the trip".
6. Point the card at the new trip page: highlight "See the trip", click the link button in the small bar of buttons, choose **Page**, and pick your new trip page. If the trip name or the photo is a link too, do the same for it.

   Careful: a copied card still goes to the old trip. Always change every link on it.

### Part D: Add the same card on your Services & Trips page

1. Go to your Services & Trips page and find "Upcoming Trips".
2. Do steps 2 to 6 of Part C again. Here, "Don't see what you want?" always stays last.

### Part E: Check, publish and test

1. Do the check in section 4. In **Preview**, click the new card's links on Home and on Services & Trips. Both must open the new trip page.
2. **Publish** (section 5).
3. On your phone, open the new trip page and tap your phone number. Your number should come up, ready to call. (You don't need to actually call.)
4. Send yourself a test from the trip's form (section 13, part C).


## 7. Change spots left, dates or price

**Spots left**

1. Open your editor and go to the trip's page (**Site Navigation**, then the trip's name).
2. In "Quick facts", change the number after "Spots left:".
3. **Publish**.

If the trip is now full, do section 8 instead.

**A trip's dates or price changed**

These show up in more than one place, so change every one:

1. **On the trip's page:** "Quick facts" (the dates, price, deposit and book-by date), and the "Trip: ..." line just above the form.
2. **In the form on that page:** click the form, click **Edit Form Fields**, click **Trip**, and change the dates in its one choice.
3. **On your Home page** ("Where are we going next?") **and your Services & Trips page** ("Upcoming Trips"): the dates and the price on this trip's card.
4. If you typed the dates under **Get found on Google** for this page, change them there too. To get there: **Site Navigation**, point at the trip's page, click its gear (as in section 6, Part A).
5. Check (section 4) and **Publish** (section 5).


## 8. Mark a trip sold out

When a trip is full, its page stays up, but the booking form becomes a waitlist. You only change words. Nothing gets deleted.

**On the trip's page:**

1. **Small label at the top:** change "Spots open" to "Sold out: join the waitlist".
2. **Quick facts:** change the "Spots left" line to "Spots left: Sold out (waitlist open)".
3. **Both Save my spot buttons:** change their words to "Join the waitlist". Don't change where they go.
4. **The form at the bottom:** change its heading "Save my spot" to "Join the waitlist".
5. Click the form. In the panel on the right:
   1. Change the send button's words to "Join the waitlist".
   2. Click **Edit Form Fields**, click **Trip**, and add "(waitlist)" to the end of the choice. For example: "Italy 2027, June 1 to 10, 2027 (waitlist)". This tells you a message is a waitlist request, not a booking.
   3. Change the message people see after they press Send to:

      You're on the list! I'll let you know as soon as a spot opens up. There's nothing to pay. If it's urgent, call me at (914) 559-8159.
6. **Under the form:** change "No payment yet. I'll be in touch to confirm your spot and explain payment." to "No payment. I'll let you know as soon as a spot opens up."

**On your Home page ("Where are we going next?") and your Services & Trips page ("Upcoming Trips"):**

7. On this trip's card, change "Spots open" to "Sold out: join the waitlist".

**Then:**

8. Check (section 4) and **Publish** (section 5).
9. Send yourself a test from the waitlist form (section 13, part C). The email should show "(waitlist)".

**If a spot opens up again,** change the words back:

- "Spots open": the label at the top, and on both cards.
- "Spots left: 1" (or however many there are).
- "Save my spot": both buttons, the form's heading and the send button.
- Take "(waitlist)" off the end of the Trip choice.
- Under the form: "No payment yet. I'll be in touch to confirm your spot and explain payment."
- The message after Send: "Got it! Your request to save a spot is on its way to me. There's nothing to pay yet. I'll be in touch [TO FILL: how fast Milena replies, e.g. within a day] to confirm your spot and explain payment. If it's urgent, call me at (914) 559-8159."


## 9. Take down a finished trip

When a trip is over, take it off your lists of upcoming trips. Keep its page for about a year, so links you've sent people still work. Then delete it.

### Part A: Remove the trip's cards (always do this)

1. Make a safety copy first (section 14, part A).
2. Go to your Home page and find "Where are we going next?".
3. Click the section. In the panel on the right, find this trip's card and click **Delete**. Delete only that trip's card. "More trips are on the way" always stays.
4. Go to your Services & Trips page, find "Upcoming Trips", and do the same. "Don't see what you want?" always stays.

### Part B: Turn the trip page into a "wrapped up" page

1. Go to the trip's page (**Site Navigation**, then the trip's name).
2. **Small label at the top:** change it to "This trip has wrapped up".
3. **The teaser line at the top:** change it to "This one has wrapped up. But I'm always planning the next adventure. Come see where we're going next."
4. **The Save my spot button at the top:** change its words to "See my upcoming trips", and set it to go to your Services & Trips page.
5. **Quick facts:** delete the "Spots left" and "Book by" lines. Then click the **Save my spot** button under the list and delete it.
6. **The form at the bottom:** click the Save my spot form section, open the section's menu, and click **Delete**. If any of the form's lines are still on the page afterwards (like "Booking for Mom or Dad?" or "No payment yet..."), delete them too.
7. Check (section 4) and **Publish** (section 5).

**In a hurry?** You can skip Part B and go straight to Part C. The only difference: anyone who saved the trip's link will see a "page not found" message instead of your friendly note.

### Part C: About a year later, delete the page

Your website can hold up to 50 pages, so old trip pages need to go eventually.

1. Make a safety copy (section 14, part A).
2. In **Site Navigation**, point at the trip's page, click its gear, and click **Delete Page**.
3. **Publish**.

Never delete **Trip template**.

## 12. Change your phone number or email

Your phone number and email are in quite a few places. Change every one, **and the link behind it too**. Otherwise people who tap the number would still be calling the old one.

**Where your phone number is**

- The bottom of every page. Change it once there, and it changes on every page.
- The button at the very top of your pages, if it says "Call (914) 559-8159".
- About page: "Milena at a glance".
- Services & Trips page: under the trip-idea form ("Rather talk? Call me at ...").
- Contact page: under "Call me", and under the form.
- Every trip page, and Trip template: "Booking for Mom or Dad?" above the form, and "Rather talk?" under it.
- The message people see after they press Send, on every form: Contact, Services & Trips, every trip page, and Trip template.

**Where your email is**

- The bottom of every page.
- About page: "Milena at a glance".
- Contact page: under "Email me".
- **Send Message To** on every form: Contact, Services & Trips, every trip page, and Trip template. This decides where messages from your website go, so don't miss one. To change it, click the form, then change **Send Message To** in the panel on the right.

**How to change a phone number and its link**

1. Click the number and type the new one.
2. Highlight the new number. In the small bar of buttons, click the link button.
3. Choose **Phone** and type your new number.

   If there's no **Phone** choice and the box only takes a web address, type tel:+1 and then your new 10-digit number, with no spaces or dashes. For example, for (914) 559-8159 you'd type: tel:+19145598159
4. Save the link.

**How to change your email and its link**

Follow the same steps, but choose **Email** and type your new address. If there's no **Email** choice, type mailto: and then your address, with no spaces. For example: mailto:milena@onyxworldtravel.com

**When you're done**

1. **Publish** (section 5).
2. On your phone, open your website and tap the phone number at the bottom: your new number should come up, ready to call. Tap your email address: a new email to your new address should open.
3. Send yourself a test from the Contact page (section 13, part C) and check that it arrives at your new email.


## 13. Read and answer messages from your website

Every form on your website sends what people type straight to your email, milena@onyxworldtravel.com. When someone presses Send, it really reaches you.

### Part A: Where messages arrive

- **In your email.** Each message comes from GoDaddy, from an address ending in mail.conversations.godaddy.com. It lists each question and the answer the person typed.
- **In GoDaddy, in a list called Conversations.** Every message is also kept there, so nothing gets lost. To open it: sign in, go to **My Products**, click **Manage** next to your website, then click **Conversations**.

How to tell which form a message came from:

- **Trip booking:** it has a "Trip" line with the trip's name.
- **Waitlist:** the "Trip" line ends in "(waitlist)".
- **Custom trip idea:** it has "Where would you like to go?".
- **Contact page:** it has "Message".

The first time, check your Spam or Junk folder too. If a message is in there, mark it "Not spam" and save the sender to your contacts, so the next ones land in your inbox.

### Part B: Answer a message

1. Open the email.
2. Click **Reply to Conversation** in the email, type your answer, and send it.
3. If they'd rather talk, or only left a phone number, just call or text them.

Your website never takes payments. When someone asks to save a spot, you get in touch about the deposit and payment yourself, the way you always do.

### Part C: Send yourself a test

Do this after you add a trip, change a form or change your email, and once a month just to be safe.

1. On your phone, open the page with the form (for a trip, the trip's own page).
2. Fill in the form with your own name and the word TEST, and press Send.
3. You should see a thank-you message on the page.
4. Check that the email arrives at milena@onyxworldtravel.com. Look in Spam or Junk too.
5. If it doesn't arrive: open that form in your editor, type your email address into **Send Message To** again, **Publish**, and test once more. Still nothing? Call GoDaddy (section 16).
6. Delete the test message.

## 14. Make a safety copy, and undo a mistake

**Small mistake?** Just fix it: retype the words, or put the old photo back. If you haven't published yet, nobody has seen it.

### Part A: Make a safety copy (before big jobs)

Do this before you add a trip, take one down, or delete anything.

1. Open your editor.
2. In the panel on the right, click **Settings**, then **Site History**.
3. Click **Create Backup**.
4. Give it a name you'll recognize, like "Before Italy 2027".

GoDaddy also makes a backup by itself when you publish or delete a section. And there's one called "Launch" that Xavier made the day your website went live.

### Part B: Go back to a safety copy

1. Open your editor. In the panel on the right, click **Settings**, then **Site History**.
2. Point at the backup you want, without clicking, and click **Restore**.
3. The page reloads. Click **Preview** and check that it looks right.
4. Click **Publish**.

Please know: going back undoes **everything** you changed after that backup was made, not only the mistake. Photos you uploaded are kept. If you're not sure which backup to pick, call GoDaddy first (section 16).


## 15. What you can ignore

GoDaddy shows lots of offers and extras. You don't need any of them for your website.

- **Anything called Airo,** and any "AI", "Generate" or "write it for me" buttons. That's GoDaddy's automatic helper. Just close it. Your own words are what your travelers love.
- **Never start a new website.** If GoDaddy offers to build, create or start a site, say no. That would be a brand-new, separate site, not yours. Your website is the one under **Websites + Marketing**.
- **Try a new look,** and the other design choices under **Theme**. They change the look of your whole website. Leave them alone.
- **Upgrade offers, add-ons and paid extras,** including offers to have someone design your site. Your plan already has everything your website needs.
- **Online store, appointments, pay buttons, sign-up boxes, chat and social media.** These are switched off on purpose. Turning one on could add a button that doesn't do anything useful for your travelers.
- **"Coming soon" pages.** You don't need one. Your website is already live.

About your bill: the price can go up when your plan renews. That's normal at GoDaddy. If a charge looks wrong, call GoDaddy.
